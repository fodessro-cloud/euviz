Market hours, days

Published May 3, 2015

Forex (Currencies) – Sunday-Friday 5:00 pm – 4:00 pm ET

Stocks – Monday-Friday 9:30 am – 4:00 pm ET, pre-market 4:00 am – 9:30 am ET, after-hours 4:00 pm – 8:00 pm ET

Commodities:

Natural gas, Crude oil, Heating oil  – open outcry (NYMEX):  Monday-Friday 9:00 am – 2:30 pm ET, electronic (Globex): Sunday-Friday 6:00 pm – 5:15 pm ET

Gold – open outcry (NYMEX): Monday-Friday 8:20 am-1:30 pm ET, electronic (Globex): Sunday-Friday 6:00 pm – 5:15 pm ET

Silver – open outcry (NYMEX): Monday-Friday 8:25 am-1:25 pm ET, electronic (Globex): Sunday-Friday 6:00 am – 5:15 pm ET

Platinum – open outcry (NYMEX): Monday-Friday 8:20 am – 1:05 pm ET, electronic (CME Globex): Sunday-Friday 6:00 pm – 5:15 pm ET

Copper – open outcry (NYMEX): Monday-Friday 8:10 am – 1:00 pm ET, electronic (Globex): Sunday-Friday 6:00 pm – 5:15 pm ET

Cocoa – open outcry (ICE): Monday-Friday 4:45 am – 1:30 pm ET, electronic (Globex): Sunday-Friday 6:00 pm – 5:15 pm ET

Coffee – open outcry (ICE): Monday-Friday 4:15 am – 1:30 pm ET, electronic (Globex): Sunday-Friday 6:00 pm – 5:15 pm ET

Live cattle, feeder cattle – open outcry (NYMEX): Monday-Friday 9:05 am – 1:00 pm CT, electronic (Globex): Monday-Friday 9:05 am – 1:55 pm CT, At 4:00 pm CT on Monday- Thursday, the markets halt and restart at 8:00 am CT on the next morning.

Oats, Soybeans – open outcry (NYMEX): Monday-Friday 8:30 am – 1:15 pm CT, electronic (Globex): Sunday-Friday 7:00 pm – 7:45 CT am and  8:30 am – 1:15 pm CT

Lumber – open outcry: Monday-Friday 9:00 am – 1:05 pm CT, electronic: Monday-Friday 9:00 am – 1:55 pm CT
