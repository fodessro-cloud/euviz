What you earn today, after inflation

Real return = nominal yield or 12-month change minus current annual inflation (HICP). Deposit rates are averages of new bank deposits by households; the best offers on the market are higher.

How we calculate it

  • Term deposit: money rolled over in a deposit fixed for up to one year at the average rate banks paid households each month.
  • Savings account: the same with the average overnight deposit rate.
  • Government bonds: interest of a 10-year government bond plus the change in its price when yields move (rising yields push prices down, as in 2022).
  • Stocks, gold, property: change in price only, in euros.
  • Inflation: the Harmonised Index of Consumer Prices; "real" means in today's purchasing power.
