      How to read it

      • Price change: all dwellings, new and existing, in the local currency.
      • After inflation: the same change in today's purchasing power.
      • Price vs. incomes: house prices divided by household disposable income per person, compared with its average since 2000.
      • Price vs. rents: house prices divided by rents, compared with its average since 2000. High values mean buying is dear compared with renting.
      • Country averages hide big differences between cities and regions.
